# Hetessy Odontologia — Specialty acquisition, free evaluation and urgency routing

**Industry:** Multidisciplinary dentistry · Mooca, São Paulo
**Audience:** Patients seeking dental evaluation across specialties
**Author's role:** Specialty strategy, landing, paid media, pain- and urgency-based routing, CRM and AI automation — led and executed directly.
**Presentation period:** February 2026

## Context & challenge

**Patients described pain, not specialties.**

Hetessy Odontologia is a multidisciplinary clinic with more than 30 years of history, at R. do Oratório, 1606, Mooca, São Paulo. The supplied site is hetessyodontologia.com.br. Its services include general dentistry, periodontics, orthodontics, prosthetics, endodontics, oral and maxillofacial surgery and implants.
A new patient often could not identify the appropriate specialty, so inquiries needed a clearer route from symptoms or urgency to an evaluation.

## Action & execution

**Route by need and urgency.**

Google Search covered local dentist, dental implant and orthodontic intent; Meta and Instagram supported discovery. Referrals and Google Maps completed the supplied mix: Search 44%, Meta / Instagram 30%, referral 16%, Maps 10%.
The landing made specialties and the team visible, then used “Book free evaluation” rather than “Contact us”. The workflow qualified specialty interest and pain urgency, prioritizing urgent inquiries before booking and CRM follow-up. Its AI and messaging steps are illustrated process design, not evidence of live integrations.

- **Search:** Service and neighborhood intent around Mooca.
- **CRO:** Clear specialties and a free-evaluation action.
- **Routing:** Pain urgency before routine specialty matching.
- **Response:** Target below one hour for the first reply.

## Result & impact

**A clearer evaluation-to-treatment route.**

The supplied February 2026 presentation dashboard shows 540 leads, 312 free evaluations, 198 treatment plans and 134 treatments started. It models cost per lead moving from R$ 72 to R$ 54.
The funnel counts above follow the case data. Some derived percentage labels in the artwork do not reconcile with those counts and should not be used as reported conversion rates.

**Illustrative KPI view:** 540 leads · 312 evaluations · 198 treatment plans · 134 started treatments

## Team, partners & LATAM

**Make urgent follow-up visible.**

The supplied view shows a 42-minute average first response against a target below one hour, with 14 referrals from general dentists and pediatricians. The team board brings referral ownership and patient follow-up into one view.
Portuguese formed the base; Spanish and English messaging was prepared for international patients in São Paulo. The specialty-routing method could be adapted elsewhere, but no broader deployment is claimed. Patient and staff names shown in the board are sample artwork.
