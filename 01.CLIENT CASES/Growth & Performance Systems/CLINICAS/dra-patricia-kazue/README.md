# Dra. Patrícia Kazue — Medical search, trust-led landing and consultation journey

**Industry:** Ophthalmology and oculoplastic surgery · São Paulo
**Audience:** Patients considering eyelid, orbit and tear-duct care
**Author's role:** Medical GTM strategy, trust-led landing, paid media, patient qualification, CRM and AI automation — led and executed directly.
**Presentation period:** October 2025

## Context & challenge

**Trust came before the click.**

Dra. Patrícia Kazue’s practice at Av. Brigadeiro Luís Antônio, 2909, Jardim Paulista, São Paulo, focuses on ophthalmology and oculoplastic care for eyelids, orbit and tear ducts. The supplied site is patriciakazue.com.br.
For a high-trust, high-consideration specialty, a prospective patient needed medical credibility and a clear explanation before booking. Anxiety about surgery made a generic appointment CTA insufficient.

## Action & execution

**Explain first, qualify second.**

Google Search captured active intent for blepharoplasty, ptosis and oculoplastic surgery in São Paulo. Meta and Instagram broadened discovery; physician referrals and Doctoralia supplied additional paths. The supplied channel view is Search 46%, Meta / Instagram 28%, referring doctors 18% and Doctoralia 8%.
The landing placed credentials, explanatory content and consented before-and-after material near the consultation action. “Book consultation + screening 15 min” won over a generic booking CTA in the supplied CRO notes.
Qualification considered procedure interest and urgency before handoff. The workflow visual describes WhatsApp follow-up, booking and CRM tracking as a process design; it is not proof of live third-party integration.

- **Search:** Procedure-specific queries and explanatory medical content.
- **Trust:** Credentials and consented visual material before the CTA.
- **CRO:** Consultation plus 15-minute screening.
- **Handoff:** Procedure interest, urgency and next action.

## Result & impact

**From inquiry to consultation decision.**

The supplied October 2025 presentation dashboard shows 410 leads, 238 consultations, 142 proposals and 96 surgeries. It models cost per lead moving from R$ 145 to R$ 108.
The case frames the operational effect as a clearer path from search intent to medically appropriate consultation and follow-up; it does not infer clinical efficacy or patient outcomes.

**Illustrative KPI view:** 410 leads · 238 consultations · 142 proposals · 96 surgeries

The dashboard labels these October 2025 figures illustrative presentation data, not verified historical clinical results. Costs are shown in BRL.

## Team, partners & LATAM

**Make the next step reassuring and owned.**

The supplied operating view shows an average first response of 1.9 hours against a target below two hours and 22 referrals from ophthalmologists and dermatologists. The team board visualizes partner and task ownership.
Portuguese formed the base; Spanish and English messaging was prepared for international patients seeking care in São Paulo. The triage and consultation pattern could be adapted to other specialists, but no other implementation is claimed. Names in the visual are fictional sample content.
