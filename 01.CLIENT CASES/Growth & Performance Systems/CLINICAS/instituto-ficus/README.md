# Instituto Ficus — Neurosurgery search, credibility and consultation qualification

**Industry:** Neurosurgery · Santana, São Paulo
**Audience:** Patients seeking neurosurgical consultation and clarity
**Author's role:** Medical GTM, credibility-led landing, paid media, condition- and insurance-based qualification, CRM and AI automation — led and executed directly.
**Presentation period:** August 2024

## Context & challenge

**A long decision needed clarity and care.**

Instituto Ficus, associated in the supplied brief with Dr. Felipe Azevedo Figueira, offers neurosurgical consultations related to the spine and pituitary conditions. Its supplied site is institutoficus.com.br; the clinic is on R. Conselheiro Saraiva in Santana, São Paulo.
Patients may arrive anxious and carrying a medical report. The commercial journey needed a welcoming first reply, clear next steps and an insurance check, without promotional treatment promises.

## Action & execution

**Lead with information and qualification.**

Google Search captured condition and specialist intent, including searches for a neurosurgeon in São Paulo, spine surgery and pituitary tumors. Referring doctors, Meta and Doctoralia formed the remaining channels. The supplied mix is Search 52%, referrals 28%, Meta 12% and Doctoralia 8%.
The landing put credentials, treated conditions and insurance questions ahead of the consultation CTA. “Book consultation + insurance check” won over a generic “Book” action in the supplied CRO notes.
Qualification mapped condition, urgency and insurance before scheduling and CRM handoff. The supplied workflow is an illustrated process design; it does not establish a live booking, WhatsApp or CRM integration.

- **Search:** Condition-specific intent and medical explanation.
- **Trust:** Credentials, conditions and insurance FAQ.
- **CRO:** Consultation plus insurance check.
- **Qualification:** Condition and urgency before the next action.

## Result & impact

**Consultation context stayed visible.**

The supplied August 2024 presentation dashboard shows 280 leads, 168 consultations, 92 proposals and 58 procedures. It models cost per lead moving from R$ 180 to R$ 132.
The operational objective was to connect search intent to an informed consultation, with condition and insurance details ready for the clinical team. The case does not make a claim about clinical outcomes.

**Illustrative KPI view:** 280 leads · 168 consultations · 92 proposals · 58 procedures

The August 2024 dashboard marks its numbers as illustrative presentation data, not verified historical clinical results. Costs are in BRL.

## Team, partners & LATAM

**Give referrals a clear next owner.**

The supplied operating view shows an average first response of 2.2 hours against a target below three hours and 16 referrals from neurologists and general practitioners. The team board illustrates task and physician-referral ownership.
Portuguese was the base. Spanish and English messaging was prepared for international patients seeking care in São Paulo. The qualification pattern could be adapted by other specialties; no wider deployment is claimed. Names and operational details in the board are sample artwork.
