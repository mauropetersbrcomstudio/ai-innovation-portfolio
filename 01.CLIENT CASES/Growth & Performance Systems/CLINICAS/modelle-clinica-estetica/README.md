# Modelle Clínica Estética — Local acquisition, free evaluations and treatment journey

**Industry:** Aesthetic medicine · Moema, São Paulo
**Audience:** Facial, body and post-operative care patients
**Author's role:** Strategy, copy, landing structure, paid media, AI automation and team and partner management — led and executed directly.
**Presentation period:** March 2024

## Context & challenge

**A known clinic needed a dependable next step.**

Modelle Clínica Estética has operated in Moema since 2004, offering facial, body and post-operative aesthetic services. Its site is esteticamodelle.com.br, and the clinic is at Av. Jamaris, 512, São Paulo.
The supplied brief describes a base of more than 4,000 registered clients. Acquisition relied on referrals and Instagram, while free evaluations too often ended without a treatment plan.

## Action & execution

**Turn interest into an evaluation.**

Google Search captured local treatment intent, including searches for skin cleansing, lymphatic drainage and laser hair removal around Moema and São Paulo. Meta and Instagram supported discovery, while referrals and WhatsApp provided direct routes. The supplied channel mix is Meta / Instagram 42%, Google Search 33%, referrals 15% and WhatsApp 10%.
The landing sequence moved from a clear hero to facial, body and post-operative services, then proof, FAQ and an evaluation action. “Book free evaluation” won over “Learn more”; social proof above plans also won in the supplied CRO notes. The reported winning creative paired before-and-after material with the free evaluation.
The lead workflow mapped capture, qualification, WhatsApp follow-up, booking and CRM visibility. The supplied visual shows a process design; it does not establish a live external integration.

- **Search intent:** Local queries by service and neighborhood.
- **Conversion:** Free-evaluation CTA and proof before plans.
- **Follow-up:** A defined owner and first-response target below two hours.
- **Partners:** Dermatology and post-operative surgical referrals.

## Result & impact

**A visible evaluation-to-care funnel.**

The supplied March 2024 presentation view shows 620 leads, 310 evaluations, 178 proposals and 112 treatments. It models cost per lead moving from R$ 85 to R$ 62.
These counts illustrate how the journey could be read across acquisition, evaluation, proposal and treatment; they should not be cited as verified historical patient outcomes.

**Illustrative KPI view:** 620 leads · 310 evaluations · 178 proposals · 112 treatments

The dashboard identifies its March 2024 figures as illustrative, simulated presentation data. All monetary amounts are in BRL.

## Team, partners & LATAM

**Keep the handoff accountable.**

The supplied operating view shows an average first response of 1.6 hours against a target below two hours and 18 referrals from dermatologists and post-operative surgeons. The team view places follow-up tasks and partner ownership together.
Portuguese was the base journey; Spanish and English messaging was prepared for international patients in São Paulo. The evaluation and follow-up structure could be adapted by other LATAM clinics, but no rollout elsewhere is claimed. Names and operational details in the board are sample artwork.
