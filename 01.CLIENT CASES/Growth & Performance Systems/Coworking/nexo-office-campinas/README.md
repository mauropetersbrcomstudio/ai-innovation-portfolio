# Nexo Office Campinas — B2B acquisition, qualified tours and SDR handoff

**Industry:** B2B coworking · Campinas
**Audience:** Expanding companies with teams of 3–15
**Author's role:** GTM strategy, B2B landing, paid media, SDR cadence, CRM rules and AI qualification — led and executed directly.
**Presentation period:** November 2026

## Context & challenge

**Demand needed a pipeline.**

Nexo Office Campinas served expanding companies looking for rooms for teams of three to fifteen. Leads arrived without a consistent path through qualification, tour, proposal and sales ownership.
The B2B page set out team-size options and a clear “Talk to sales” action, connecting company intent to a suitable tour.

## Action & execution

**Qualify before the tour.**

Google Search captured B2B intent around “sala para equipe Campinas” and “escritório para empresa Campinas”. LinkedIn addressed company decision makers; Meta extended reach. A fuller qualification form won on qualified tours over a short form.
The SDR cadence combined day-zero email and WhatsApp, a day-one call and a day-three follow-up. The CRM handoff required team size, start date and need; AI-assisted scoring and tagging supported prioritization.

- **Acquisition:** Google Search B2B 38%, LinkedIn 34%, Meta 28% in the supplied channel view.
- **CRO:** Full qualification form and “Talk to sales” CTA.
- **Cadence:** Day 0 email and WhatsApp; Day 1 call; Day 3 follow-up.
- **Handoff:** Team size, start date and need recorded before sales transfer.

## Result & impact

**The full funnel became visible.**

The presentation view connects lead capture, qualification, tours, proposals and contracts. It shows an average deal of R$ 7,170, an 18-day cycle and a 1.8-hour SDR response against a target below two hours.
The contract mix shown is 45% for teams of 3–5, 35% for 6–10 and 20% for 11–15.

**Illustrative KPI view:** 840 leads · 310 qualified · 185 tours · 68 contracts

November 2026 figures are illustrative presentation data, not verified historical client performance. All monetary amounts are in BRL.

## Team, partners & LATAM

**A clear owner for each opportunity.**

Portuguese formed the base journey. Spanish messaging addressed LATAM companies opening a branch; English supported technology headquarters abroad. SDR qualification in PT, ES and EN was structured to pass a complete summary to sales.
The team board illustrates account ownership, stages and partner associations. It contains sample names and dates outside November 2026; those details are presentation artwork, not client records.
