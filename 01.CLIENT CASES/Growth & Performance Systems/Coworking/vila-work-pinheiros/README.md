# Vila Work Pinheiros — Local acquisition, handoff and commercial alignment

**Industry:** Neighborhood coworking · Pinheiros
**Audience:** Local professionals and small teams
**Author's role:** Commercial alignment, landing, paid media, CRM rules, SLA design and partner management — led and executed directly.
**Presentation period:** November 2026

## Context & challenge

**A strong location needed a shared sales process.**

Vila Work Pinheiros had a compelling location, but marketing, front desk and sales applied different lead criteria and responded at different speeds.
The landing page made the neighborhood advantage concrete with the “5 min from subway” hook, plan information, FAQ and a direct WhatsApp route.

## Action & execution

**Align criteria, routing and response.**

Meta Local supplied 49% of the illustrated leads, Google Search 32% and partners 19%. The FAQ plus WhatsApp CTA won over a page without FAQ; WhatsApp performed better than a form for local intent.
Lead capture, qualification, automatic owner distribution and breach rerouting were defined around a response target below three hours. A handoff summary kept the next representative informed.

- **Paid channels:** Meta Local and Google Search, supported by partners.
- **CRO:** FAQ and WhatsApp CTA for local intent.
- **CRM:** Shared lead criteria and automatic owner distribution.
- **SLA:** Re-route if the response target is breached.

## Result & impact

**Handoff quality became measurable.**

The supplied November view shows 127 new leads, 32 deals won and R$ 128.4k in pipeline. It reports 142 of 151 handoffs completed, a 94% rate; that handoff denominator is a separate operational measure from new leads.
Average first response is shown at 2.1 hours, improving from 2.5 hours. Lost reasons are price 38%, timing 29%, location 18% and no response 15%.

**Illustrative KPI view:** 127 new leads · 32 deals won · 94% handoff success · 2.1h average response

November 2026 figures are illustrative presentation data, not verified historical client performance. Pipeline is shown in BRL. The handoff and new-lead counts use different denominators.

## Team, partners & LATAM

**Give every stage an owner.**

The management view brought marketing, front desk and sales into one board, with referral visibility across realtors, accountants and consultancies.
Portuguese was the base. Spanish and English messaging was prepared for international teams in Pinheiros and Vila Madalena. The board contains sample people and 2024 dates; these are presentation details, not November 2026 records.
