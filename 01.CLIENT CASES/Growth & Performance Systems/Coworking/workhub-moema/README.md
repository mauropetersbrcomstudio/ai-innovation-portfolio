# WorkHub Moema — acquisition, conversion and customer journey

**Industry:** Coworking and flexible workspaces · Moema, São Paulo  
**Author's role:** Led and executed strategy, copy, landing page wireframe, paid media, AI automation, and team and partner management directly.  
**Presentation period:** November 2026

## Context & challenge

WorkHub Moema offered private offices, desks and meeting rooms in a neighborhood coworking space. Acquisition depended entirely on word of mouth. The commercial challenge was to build a repeatable journey from local discovery to a visit, proposal and contract.

## Action & execution

- **Paid media and demand capture:** Structured Google Search around location and product intent, including “coworking Moema” and “sala privativa São Paulo”; used Meta and Instagram for local demand generation and LinkedIn for B2B private office interest.
- **Creative and conversion:** Tested a video walkthrough, static image and carousel. Reframed the landing page around the action “Book a tour / Try 1 free day” and moved social proof above plans. The page architecture linked ads, booking, visit, proposal and contract.
- **Lead operations:** Designed UTM capture, lead qualification, follow-up and CRM handoff as one journey. Set a first-response target below three hours, with human sales ownership.
- **Partners and management:** Organized a realtor and accountant referral channel and coordinated team tasks, ownership and weekly alignment.
- **Regional readiness:** Used Portuguese as the base and prepared Spanish and English messaging for international professionals and companies in São Paulo. The approach was structured for possible adaptation to other LATAM capitals; this is not a claim of deployment elsewhere.

## Result & impact

The work established a multichannel acquisition and conversion journey beyond word of mouth, with a defined response target, partner referrals and an explicit handoff from marketing to sales.

**Illustrative November 2026 KPI view supplied for presentation:** 1,248 leads → 412 tours (33.0%) → 186 proposals (45.1%) → 87 contracts (46.8% of proposals; 6.97% of leads). The dashboard models CPL moving from R$230 to R$165 (−28.3%). Its channel mix is Meta/Instagram 34.3%, Google Search 25.0%, referral 20.5%, LinkedIn 14.3% and events 5.9%. The creative comparison is video walkthrough 5.8%, static 3.2% and carousel 2.4%. The landing page visualization shows +32% for the tour/free-day CTA and +24% tour bookings after moving social proof above plans. The operations visualization shows a 2.4-hour average response, a target below three hours and 12 weekly referrals split between seven realtors and five accountants, with R$200 per qualified referral.

These figures and the sample people shown in the images are illustrative presentation data, not verified historical client performance or real-person testimonials. All monetary values shown are in BRL.
