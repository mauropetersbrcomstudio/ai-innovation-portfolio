# Spaces — Brand-consistent city journeys and Day Pass CRO

**Industry:** Design-led flexible workspace · Brazil
**Audience:** Individuals and hybrid teams
**Author's role:** Creative thesis, city and plan architecture, media logic, CRM tagging and team and partner management — led and executed directly.
**Presentation period:** November 2026

## Context & challenge

**Design drew attention. Plans needed clarity.**

Spaces appealed to individuals and hybrid teams with a creative workspace experience, but communication varied by location. Prospects attracted by the environment still needed to understand plans and pricing.
A shared city and plan architecture connected discovery, local availability, entry offer and proposal while keeping the brand consistent.

## Action & execution

**Lead with flexibility and mobility.**

Google Search captured city-level intent; Meta carried creative demand and LinkedIn addressed hybrid teams. The flexibility and mobility angle performed better than design alone for that audience.
The entry test compared Tour with Day Pass. Day Pass won at 5.1% versus 3.9% for Tour in the supplied view. AI-assisted hook variation and CRM tagging by city, plan and work style supported local routing.

- **City mix:** São Paulo 52%, Rio 28%, Brasília 12%, Belo Horizonte 8%.
- **Entry offer:** Day Pass before a higher-commitment proposal.
- **Media:** Google Search, Meta and LinkedIn by audience and city.
- **CRM:** City, plan and work-style tags with local ownership.

## Result & impact

**A lighter first step won.**

The supplied illustrative analysis shows Day Pass at 5.1% conversion to paid membership versus 3.9% for a tour, a 1.2 percentage point difference.
The dashboard also depicts a multi-city acquisition mix and plan prices in BRL. These are presentation examples, not verified historical sales or active price claims.

**Illustrative KPI view:** 5.1% Day Pass · 3.9% Tour · +1.2 pp entry difference · 4 Brazilian cities

All numerical dashboard values for November 2026 are illustrative. The team artwork shows other countries and older dates; these are sample content.

## Team, partners & LATAM

**A repeatable playbook with city owners.**

Startups, agencies and hybrid companies supported partner outreach. The operating model assigned an owner by city and defined a response target below three hours.
Portuguese formed the base, with Spanish and English messaging prepared for international hybrid teams. The All Access story addressed multi-city use; no broader LATAM rollout is claimed.
