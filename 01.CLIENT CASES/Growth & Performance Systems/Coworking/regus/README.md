# Regus — Multi-city acquisition, plan selection and CRM routing

**Industry:** Flexible workspace · multiple Brazilian cities
**Audience:** Businesses seeking offices, coworking and meeting rooms
**Author's role:** Strategy, landing architecture, media logic, CRM routing, city SLA and team and partner management — led and executed directly.
**Presentation period:** November 2026

## Context & challenge

**Choice created friction.**

Regus presented private offices, coworking, meeting rooms and virtual offices across many locations. Prospects could lose the thread between product, plan and city before a tour was booked.
The landing architecture began with the customer’s intended workspace, then asked for the city. That plan-first order won over location-first in the supplied CRO comparison.

## Action & execution

**Connect intent to the city owner.**

Google Search was structured by São Paulo, Rio and Brasília. Meta addressed broader demand and LinkedIn the B2B audience. The illustrated intent split was office 48%, coworking 32% and meeting room 20%.
The lead journey captured product and city, standardized UTM information, routed to a city owner, summarized a tour and recorded the next CRM step. Tour teams passed qualified context to location sales on the same day.

- **Search:** City-specific workspace intent.
- **CRO:** Choose plan intent, then city.
- **Routing:** Owner per city and plan type.
- **Handoff:** Tour team to location sales with qualification context.

## Result & impact

**One view across three cities.**

The illustrative November view shows 250 leads, 112 tours and 48 proposals, with a R$ 598k proposal pipeline and R$ 12,458 average proposal value.
Response times shown are São Paulo 2.4 hours, Rio 2.7 hours and Brasília 2.5 hours, against a target below three hours.

**Illustrative KPI view:** 250 leads · 112 tours · 48 proposals · R$ 598k pipeline

These November 2026 numbers are illustrative reference-case figures in BRL. The supplied dashboard explicitly says “not closed contract”; no contract outcome is claimed here.

## Team, partners & LATAM

**Local ownership within a global journey.**

Portuguese formed the core flow; Spanish and English were prepared for multinational prospects. The city-based ownership model kept a consistent funnel while allowing local sales response.
The supplied team illustration shows international cities and 2024 dates that differ from the Brazil-focused November dashboard. They are sample artwork, not evidence of Brazilian operations or historical client metrics.
