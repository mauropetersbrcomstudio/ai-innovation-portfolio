# Neon Coworking — Two funnels for coworking and event demand

**Industry:** Neighborhood coworking and events · Vila Mascote
**Audience:** Local teams, trainers and event organizers
**Author's role:** Offer hierarchy, two-funnel structure, landing pages, paid media, SDR cadence and partner management — led and executed directly.
**Presentation period:** November 2026

## Context & challenge

**Two offers competed for one message.**

Neon Coworking in Vila Mascote offered private offices, desks and an event space for workshops, conferences, training and networking. Coworking and event inquiries had different buying decisions but shared one acquisition story.
Two landing paths clarified each offer: coworking moved from plans and space to a tour; events moved from capacity and pricing to a date check.

## Action & execution

**Route by front and urgency.**

Meta Local and Google Search targeted Vila Mascote, Jabaquara and São Paulo’s South Zone for coworking. Event intent had separate Meta and Google messages for organizers, trainers and companies. Local and event-specific hooks beat generic coworking language.
Coworking used a tour as the qualified entry step. For events, a date check won over an initial quote request, reducing back-and-forth. Inquiry routing separated the fronts, with a response target below two hours for near-date events and below three hours for coworking.

- **Coworking:** Hero → Plans → Space → Tour.
- **Events:** Hero → Capacity → Pricing → Date request.
- **CRO:** Qualified tour and date-check actions.
- **Routing:** Front and urgency determine owner and SLA.

## Result & impact

**Two funnels, two clear outcomes.**

The illustrative coworking view shows 380 leads, 142 tours and 68 contracts. The event view shows 145 inquiries, 98 date checks and 54 confirmed events.
The supplied event pricing view shows R$ 290 per hour and R$ 1,050 for four hours. Those amounts are presentation values and should not be treated as a current price quote.

**Illustrative KPI view:** 380 coworking leads · 68 coworking contracts · 145 event inquiries · 54 events confirmed

November 2026 funnel numbers and pricing are illustrative presentation data, not verified historical client results or current public prices. Amounts are in BRL.

## Team, partners & LATAM

**Partners supported both fronts.**

Local trainers, event producers and South Zone businesses formed the partner base. The board assigned tasks and calendar ownership across coworking and event work.
Portuguese was the base and Spanish and English messaging was prepared. The two-funnel logic could be adapted to other neighborhood hubs, but no other-city deployment is claimed. Sample names and October 2024 dates in the board are artwork details.
