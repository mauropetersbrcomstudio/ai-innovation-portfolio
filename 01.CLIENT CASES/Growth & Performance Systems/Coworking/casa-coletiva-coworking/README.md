# Casa Coletiva Coworking — post-visit conversion and follow-up

**Industry:** Coworking and flexible workspaces  
**Author's role:** Led and executed strategy, copy, the post-visit landing journey, paid media, AI-assisted follow-up and team management directly.  
**Presentation period:** November 2026

## Context & challenge

Casa Coletiva Coworking serves a community of freelancers and designers. Visits were arriving, but too few became contracts. Follow-up after a visit lacked a defined sequence, making it difficult to turn interest in the space and community into a tailored proposal and decision.

## Action & execution

- **Paid acquisition:** Used Meta and Instagram, Google Search and referrals to reach local prospects. The creative hook, “Working alone is tiring”, led with the value of belonging to a working community.
- **Landing page and CRO:** Built a post-visit journey that reconnects a visit to a personalized proposal, clear plans, social proof, benefits and a membership action. Compared a full plan comparison with a personalized proposal.
- **Sales follow-up:** Defined visit capture, a visit summary, tailored message, a 48-hour reminder, CRM update and sales-team visibility. AI supports drafting and routine follow-up within this commercial journey.
- **Partners and management:** Organized relationships with creative collectives, design schools and event producers, with team ownership for follow-up tasks.
- **Regional readiness:** Portuguese was the base, with Spanish and English messaging prepared for foreign freelancers and nomads. The approach could be adapted to other LATAM hubs; no deployment outside this case is claimed.

## Result & impact

The work gave the team a clearer path from visit to proposal and contract, with a response target and same-day follow-up measure. The post-visit page and personalized proposal gave the visitor a more direct next step.

**Illustrative November 2026 KPI view supplied for presentation:** 220 visits → 148 proposals (67.3% of visits) → 96 contracts (64.9% of proposals; 43.6% of visits). The channel mix shown is Meta/Instagram 38%, Google Search 32% and referrals 30%. The comparison shows a 62% win rate for the personalized proposal versus 44% for the full comparison, an 18 percentage-point difference. Follow-up is shown at an average of 3.1 hours against a target below four hours, with 92% on the same day.

These figures are illustrative presentation data, not verified historical client performance. The supplied artwork includes sample names, testimonials and operational details; they should not be read as verified client records. One team illustration uses dates outside November 2026. Monetary figures depicted in the artwork are in BRL.
