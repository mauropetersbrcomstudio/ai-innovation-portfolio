# Playinwork — Positioning clarity, acquisition and lead qualification

**Industry:** Community-led coworking
**Audience:** Freelancers, creators and small teams
**Author's role:** Creative thesis, landing definition, media logic, qualification and team and partner management — led and executed directly.
**Presentation period:** November 2026

## Context & challenge

**The playful promise needed a clear definition.**

Playinwork offered a boutique, community-led place for freelancers, creators and small teams. “Play” made the brand memorable but could be read as leisure rather than a structured place to work.
The landing hero defined the offer in the first few seconds, then invited a tour and explained plans, amenities and community.

## Action & execution

**Clarity outperformed curiosity.**

Meta and Instagram reached creators; Google captured coworking intent. A clarity-first hook won at 6.2% against 3.8% for curiosity in the supplied test.
A definition-first hero — “Work seriously, in a space that feels alive” — won over community-first. Qualification asked about work need before community fit; if work need was missing, the lead entered nurture instead of sales. AI-assisted hook testing, tagging and follow-up supported that operating sequence.

- **Paid media:** Meta / Instagram for creators; Google for active intent.
- **Creative test:** Clarity 6.2% versus curiosity 3.8%.
- **Landing:** Definition first, community proof next.
- **Qualification:** Work need before community fit.

## Result & impact

**Positioning connected to the funnel.**

The illustrative November view shows 520 leads moving to 198 tours, 92 proposals and 54 contracts. It models an overall lead-to-contract conversion of about 10.4%.
The same view keeps the creative hook comparison beside the funnel, linking message clarity to sales qualification.

**Illustrative KPI view:** 520 leads · 198 tours · 92 proposals · 54 contracts

All November 2026 figures are illustrative presentation data, not verified historical client performance. Monetary amounts shown in the artwork are in BRL.

## Team, partners & LATAM

**Community fit comes after work fit.**

Creators, communities and event producers supported discovery and partnerships. The team board illustrated partner ownership, events and follow-up tasks.
Portuguese was the base, with Spanish and English messaging prepared for remote workers and creators. The board’s sample people and earlier dates are presentation artwork, not November 2026 records.
